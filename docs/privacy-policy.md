# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

This policy explains what the Zeke Discord bot ("Zeke") stores and why.

## What Zeke stores

- **Discord IDs:**
  - your Discord user ID, if you link a Riot account (`/link`) or set a preference (`/mode`)
  - server IDs, for servers that use leaderboards (`/track`)
- **Riot account data:** Riot ID (name and tag), region and Riot's account identifier (PUUID) for accounts that are linked, tracked or looked up.
- **Game data:** ranked match results, champion stats, rank and LP snapshots, and LP changes, taken from the public Riot Games API, along with cached match details.
- **Preferences:** your default queue (Solo/Duo or Flex).

Zeke does **not** read or store message content beyond the commands you send it. It doesn't store email addresses or passwords, and it has no access to your Riot or Discord account login.

## Why

Only to provide the bot's features: profiles, match scoreboards, recaps, LP graphs, leaderboards and scouting.

## Sharing

The data is **not sold or shared** with third parties. It's shown only in Discord, to people who use Zeke's commands. The bot talks to the Riot Games API, Data Dragon / CommunityDragon (for images) and Discord. It runs on a cloud host (Railway), where the database is stored.

## Retention and deletion

Data is kept for as long as the bot runs, so that history (graphs, recaps) keeps working.
- `/unlink` removes your Discord ↔ Riot link at any time.
- To have an account's stored games and history deleted completely, ask in the [Zeke Discord server](https://discord.gg/Zf6zYWEJNK) or open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/qpcic/zeke).

## Children

Zeke is meant for users who meet Discord's minimum age requirements.
